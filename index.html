ZipifyTunes

Playlist CSVs in, tagged MP3s out: as a ZIP, or straight onto a folder or pen drive.

Organize your songs → sort into folders, rename, make playlists, clean duplicates

Set up

Pick one or more. Each CSV becomes its own playlist, downloaded one after another.

Up to 20. More at once is faster, but YouTube may refuse some downloads when too many run together; those land in the review list.

Leave this on "Don't use" unless downloads fail with "confirm you're not a bot". Then pick the browser where you're signed in to YouTube. Chrome, Brave and Edge need your Mac's keychain password (usually your login password) each time unless you choose Always Allow; Safari needs Full Disk Access for this app instead.

Click Choose… to pick a folder or pen drive (or type a path). Each playlist goes into its own folder there, named after its CSV.
