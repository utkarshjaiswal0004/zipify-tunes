Organize your songs

Scan a folder or pen drive, narrow the list down, then sort, rename, make a playlist or clean out duplicates. You see every change before it happens, and you can undo it afterwards.

Folder

Click Choose… (or a drive below) to pick a folder or pen drive and read its songs, or type its full path and click Scan. Songs in the folders inside it are included. Scanning only reads; nothing changes until you apply a plan.
